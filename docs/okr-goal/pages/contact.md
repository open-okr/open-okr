## 1. Hero

**Eyebrow:** Contact

# Talk to us.

A person reads every message. Tell us what you are trying to do and we will reply within [one business day].

## 2. Choose a topic

Five tiles. Clicking one sets the form's topic and changes the form's helper text.

| Tile | Helper text on the form |
|---|---|
| **An organisation-wide rollout** | How many people, how you run OKRs today, and whether you want the cloud or your own servers |
| **Support for a self-hosted instance** | Where it runs, how many people use it, and what kind of help you want |
| **Security or procurement** | Attach your questionnaire, or tell us what your reviewers need |
| **Partnerships** | For OKR consultants and coaches. Tell us about your clients and your method |
| **Press** | Your publication and your deadline |

## 3. The form

| Field | Type | Required |
|---|---|---|
| Name | Text | Yes |
| Work email | Email | Yes |
| Organisation | Text | Yes |
| Topic | Preset from the tile, editable | Yes |
| Number of people | Choice: under 50 · 50 to 250 · 250 to 1,000 · 1,000 to 5,000 · over 5,000 | No |
| Cloud or self-hosted | Choice: Cloud · Self-hosted · Not sure yet | No |
| Message | Long text | Yes |
| Attachment | File, for a questionnaire | No |

**Button:** `[ Send ]`

**Under the button, small:** We use your details only to reply to you. [Privacy policy].

**After sending:** *Thanks. We have your message, and a person will reply within [one business day]. In the meantime, the live demo is open: `[ Open the demo ]`*
