THE PROBLEM

Three ways to run OKRs today, three ways to lose the practice

WHAT MOST START WITH

Spreadsheets

No cadence, no accountability, no quality bar. Nobody is ever told anything. The file is opened twice a quarter.

WHAT MOST BUY

Conventional OKR trackers

A database with a progress bar. They store objectives faithfully and are entirely passive. They will happily hold a badly written key result for a whole quarter without comment.

WHAT MOST FALL BACK ON

Consulting and training

The method is real, but it walks out of the door with the consultant. There is nothing to enforce it on a wet Tuesday in week six.

The practice that makes OKRs work has been well understood for twenty years. It just does not live in the software.
