The risk we are managing, openly

A coaching engine that produces false positives is worse than no coaching at all, because people learn to dismiss it.

What we are doing about it

The warn-versus-fail line on all twenty rules will be tuned against a corpus of real, anonymised OKRs before launch. Assembling that corpus is a named deliverable of the coaching phase design gate, not an afterthought.

Where a partner adds the most value

This is the single place where methodology expertise changes the product most. A partner who can challenge a threshold, or contribute real OKRs to tune against, moves the quality of every coaching message in the product.
