ALIGN AND COMMIT

Six publish gates, enforced server-side

1

Every objective has a title, a named champion and a named reviewer.

2

Every key result passes its checks.

3

Alignment is mapped. Each objective states what it contributes to.

4

Every dependency is confirmed, or logged with a named risk owner.

5

Capacity is checked, nothing is left exceeding, and the cuts are recorded.

6

A publication date is set before day one of the cycle.

Gate five is the one nobody has had

Capacity is read from the initiatives actually planned against each key result, not self-declared.

If one key result exceeds capacity and no cut is recorded, the cycle cannot be published.

A plan where nothing was cut is a plan that has not been made. Without the gate, the cut happens in week six instead, without a decision.
