AGPL-3.0, plus a contributor agreement

AGPL stops a third party selling a closed hosted version. An organisation self-hosting for its own staff takes on no obligations at all. The contributor agreement preserves the right to run a paid cloud and to relax the licence later.

Air-gap capable, by construction

No feature hard-depends on an external service. AI points at a local model or is off. Channels are optional, telemetry is opt-in, assets are self-hosted. Tenant isolation is enforced inside the database, not in application code.

Self-host is never seat-limited and never feature-gated. The cloud sells operation, not features.
