# 3. Q3 2027: the long summer

1 July to 30 September 2027. Week 1 starts on Monday 5 July. A quarter in which the organisation changes under its OKRs:
- half of Europe is on holiday;
- Support merges into Customer Success;
- the Head of Engineering goes on parental leave;
- a new team forms in week 6.

The operating margin breaches its floor and gets a recovery OKR. The SOC 2 report, the year's biggest commitment, lands.

## The margin recovery

**NW-Q3-03 · 5 Jul (W1) · Hugo · S-20 KPI grid · METHOD v2 §6.4 · P9-T17**
June's operating margin is recorded: 7.6%. That is below the red boundary of 8% for the second month running. The KPI reads unhealthy, its real band, and the grid shows its six-month trend.

**NW-Q3-04 · 6 Jul (W1) · Coach, Hugo, Elena · S-02 review inbox, S-19 recovery board · METHOD v2 §6.5, §5.3 · P9-T18**
The coach proposes a response, since it has been two consecutive unhealthy periods. The proposal lands in Hugo's review inbox with the three responses. Elena and Hugo choose a recovery OKR. The draft:
- **Committed.**
- **A qualitative objective.** "Margins we can run the business on again", with the why left to Hugo.
- **First key result:** the KPI itself, 7.6% to its healthy boundary of 13.5%.
- **Drivers.** The two leading drivers below target that have owners: expansion seats, and self-serve deflection.

The semantic review flags deflection: "These two claim the same movement", because SU1 and C2 already own it. Hugo removes it and adds "Average renewal discount 14% → 6%", the margin lever Brightline's discounting opened. He launches it on 6 July. That is inside the publication window, so C6 is part of Q3's plan, not marked as an addition.
*Test:* Given operating margin unhealthy in May and June, when June's reading is recorded, then the coach proposes, it does not create. The draft is committed, has a number-free objective, and its first key result is the KPI from 7.6 to 13.5. Given a draft driver already owned by another objective's key result, then the semantic review flags a possible double count.

**NW-Q3-05 · Jul to Sep · Hugo, Elena · S-19 recovery board, S-21 KPI detail · METHOD v2 §6.4 · P9-T17**
While C6 runs, the operating margin KPI shows its real band beside C6's progress, never a projection in place of the reading.

| Month | Operating margin | Band | C6 progress |
|---|---|---|---|
| July | 8.9% | Watch | 22% |
| August | 10.1% | Watch | 42% |
| September | 10.4% | Watch | 47% |

*Test:* Given a KPI at 8.9% with an active recovery, when the board renders, then it reads "watch" and shows the recovery's 22% beside it, not a projected value.

## The organisation changes

**NW-Q3-06 · 12 Jul to 27 Aug · Everyone in Europe · S-22 weekly session, account settings · METHOD v2 §7.4 · P9-T19, plus gap G-2**
Northwind's offices in London, Madrid, Berlin and Stockholm take their summer holidays.
- **Holidays.** The Product space marks the weeks of 9 and 16 August as holidays. No check-in is expected, and the streak does not break.
- **Personal leave.** A personal leave also needs the Champion to hold that person's nudges and route their reviews to someone else. No task builds that yet (gap G-2 in [05-scenario-index.md](05-scenario-index.md) §3). Until one does, members set quiet hours, and the coordinator checks in for a goal whose champion is away.

*Test:* Given the Product space with the weeks of 9 and 16 August marked as holidays, when no check-in is published in those weeks, then the streak continues, and no space-level nudge is sent.

**NW-Q3-07 · 26 Jul (W4) · Elena, Tomás, Kofi · S-33 people, spaces · Gap G-1**
Support merges into Customer Success, and Kofi now reports to Tomás. Moving the members is a membership change, and it works today. SU1 should move with them, but no action moves a goal from one space to another (gap G-1). Until one exists:
- SU1 stays in the Support space until it closes;
- Tomás's team coordinates it from Customer Success;
- in Q4, Support's objectives are written in Customer Success.

Writing new objectives for the merged team, rather than carrying both teams' sets, is what whatmatters advises: "A new team doesn't automatically inherit old OKRs."

*Test (once G-1 is built):* Given SU1 in Support, when it is moved to Customer Success, then its check-ins, dependencies and alignment move with it, and the activity records the move.

**NW-Q3-08 · 30 Jul · Mei, Priya · S-14 roles · METHOD v2 §2.5 · Today, plus gap G-2**
Mei's parental leave runs from 2 August to 25 October. Before she goes, she hands her roles over, and each reassignment is recorded in the activity:
- the champion of E1 and C3, and the owner of C3.1, go to Leo;
- reviewing her team's objectives goes to Priya.

*Test:* Given C3's champion reassigned to Leo, when the Champion runs, then Leo receives C3's check-in nudges, and Mei receives none.

**NW-Q3-09 · 9 Aug (W6) · Yuki, Priya · S-33 spaces, OKRs list and diagram · METHOD v2 §2.9 · P9-T07b, P9-T10, P9-T12, P9-T13**

*Test:* Given a space created on 9 August, when its first objective is created, then it is marked added mid-cycle with the required reason, and it can be aligned by dragging in the diagram.

**NW-Q3-10 · 23 Aug (W8) · Amara, Yuki · S-14 drawer · METHOD v2 §2.10, §2.9 · P9-T06 and P9-T12**
