# 3. Q3 2027: the long summer

1 July to 30 September 2027. Week 1 starts on Monday 5 July. A quarter in which the organisation changes under its OKRs:
- half of Europe is on holiday;
- Support merges into Customer Success;
- the Head of Engineering goes on parental leave;
- a new team forms in week 6.

The operating margin breaches its floor and gets a recovery OKR. The SOC 2 report, the year's biggest commitment, lands.

## Planning Q3

**NW-Q3-01 · 21 to 28 Jun · Leadership, Elena · S-05, S-09, S-36 · METHOD §8.9, §12**
Phase 2 runs after Q2's retrospective and ranks the strategic issues:
1. Brightline (impact 5).
2. Margin (5).
3. Expansion, deferred from Q2 as CS2 (4).
4. Activation (4).

Expansion stays on the list, below the two that have to come first. Kept and modified objectives pre-fill, and the company set publishes on 28 June.

Elena also sets OBJ-1 back from block to warn. In Q2 it cost a week of rewrites and one override, for little gain over the warning. The change is audited, and Q2's closed snapshot still shows the block level it was graded under.
*Test:* Given CS2 deferred at impact 4, when Phase 2 ranks the issues, then it is listed and can be ranked below others without being lost. Given OBJ-1 set back to warn, then Q2's snapshot still records it at block.

**NW-Q3-02 · 1 Jul · Daniel · S-36 space settings · METHOD §7.1, §11**
Sales deals move in fortnights, and weekly check-ins were repeating themselves. Daniel sets the Sales space's check-in frequency to every two weeks. The anchor day stays Monday, and the streak and the escalation ladders follow the new cadence.
*Test:* Given the Sales space at every two weeks, when a Sales goal is not checked in on the in-between Monday, then no nudge is sent and the streak does not break.

## The margin recovery

**NW-Q3-03 · 5 Jul (W1) · Hugo, Tomás · S-20 KPI grid · METHOD §6.4**
June's readings are recorded:
- **Operating margin: 7.6%.** Below the red boundary of 8% for the second month running.
- **Expansion seats added: 125.** Below its red boundary of 130 for the second month, because expansion was paused for most of Q2.

Both KPIs read unhealthy, their real band, with their six-month trends.
*Test:* Given operating margin at 7.8 in May and 7.6 in June with a red boundary of 8, when June's reading is recorded, then the KPI reads unhealthy for the second consecutive period.

**NW-Q3-04 · 6 Jul (W1) · Coach, Hugo, Elena, Tomás · S-02 review inbox, S-19 recovery board · METHOD §6.5, §5.3**
Two consecutive unhealthy periods make the coach propose, not create, a response for each KPI. The proposals land in the review inbox with the three responses.

**Operating margin.** Elena and Hugo choose a recovery OKR. The draft:
- committed;
- a number-free objective, "Margins we can run the business on again", with the why left to Hugo;
- its first key result is the KPI itself, 7.6% to its healthy boundary of 13.5%;
- then the leading drivers that are below target and have owners: expansion seats, tickets per account and self-serve deflection.

The semantic review flags two of them, "These two claim the same movement": tickets per account is already C2.2, and deflection is already SU1.1. Hugo removes both and adds "Average renewal discount 14% → 6%", the lever Brightline's discounting opened. C6 launches inside the publication window, so it is part of Q3's plan.

**Expansion seats.** Tomás chooses the second response, "add a key result", and records that C6.2 is that key result.
*Test:* Given two unhealthy periods, when June's reading is recorded, then the coach proposes, and the draft is committed, its objective has no number, and its first key result is the KPI from 7.6 to 13.5. Given a drafted driver already owned by another objective's key result, then the semantic review flags a possible double count (AI on).

**NW-Q3-05 · Jul to Sep · Hugo, Elena · S-19 recovery board, S-21 KPI detail · METHOD §6.4**
While C6 runs, the operating margin KPI shows its real band beside the recovery's progress, never a projection in place of the reading.

| Month (recorded early the next month) | Operating margin | Band | C6.1 progress, the KPI key result |
|---|---|---|---|
| July | 8.9% | Watch | 22% |
| August | 10.1% | Watch | 42% |

*Test:* Given a KPI at 8.9% with an active recovery, when the board renders, then it reads "watch" with the key result's 22% beside it, not a projected value.

## The organisation changes

**NW-Q3-06 · 12 Jul to 27 Aug · Product space, everyone in Europe · S-22 weekly session, space settings · METHOD §7.4**
Northwind's offices in London, Madrid, Berlin and Stockholm take their summer holidays.
- **Holidays.** On 12 July Sara marks the weeks of 9 and 16 August as holidays for the Product space. No check-in is due in those weeks, nobody is nudged for them, and the streak does not break.
- **Personal leave.** Anybody away outside those weeks marks their own leave, with a delegate. Sara is away from 23 to 27 August with Amara as her delegate: nobody nudges Sara, and Amara posts the Monday check-in on P4, the objective Sara champions.

*Test:* Given the Product space with the weeks of 9 and 16 August marked as holidays, when no check-in is published in those weeks, then the streak continues, and no nudge is sent for them. Given Sara on leave from 23 to 27 August with Amara as her delegate, when P4's check-in falls due, then Amara is nudged and Sara is not.

**NW-Q3-07 · 26 Jul (W4) · Elena, Tomás, Kofi · S-33 people, spaces, OKRs list · METHOD §2.9**
Support merges into Customer Success, and from 26 July Kofi reports to Tomás.
- **The people.** Moving the members is a membership change, and it works today.
- **The objective.** Elena moves SU1 to Customer Success from the list's row menu. Its key result, its check-ins, its dependency on Engineering and its alignment to C2 go with it, recorded as one dated change naming both spaces.
- **The next cycle.** In Q4 the merged team writes its own objectives in Customer Success, rather than carrying both teams' sets.

Writing new objectives for the merged team, rather than carrying both teams' sets, is what whatmatters advises: "A new team doesn't automatically inherit old OKRs."
*Test:* Given SU1 in Support with its check-ins and a dependency, when it is moved to Customer Success, then its check-ins, dependencies and alignment read from Customer Success, and the activity records the move. Given a member with edit on Support but not on Customer Success, then the move is refused.

**NW-Q3-08 · 30 Jul · Mei, Priya · S-14 roles, people · METHOD §2.5, §7.4**
Mei's parental leave runs from 2 August to 25 October.
- **Her roles.** Eleven weeks is long enough to hand them over, and each reassignment is recorded in the activity: the champion of E1 and C3, and the owner of C3.1, go to Leo.
- **Her leave.** She marks the leave with Priya as her delegate, so anything still addressed to her, such as reviewing her department's objectives, goes to Priya, and nobody nudges Mei.

*Test:* Given C3's champion reassigned to Leo, when the Champion runs, then Leo receives C3's check-in nudges, and Mei receives none. Given Mei on leave with Priya as her delegate, when a check-in on an Engineering objective Mei reviews is published, then Priya owes the acknowledgement.

**NW-Q3-09 · 9 Aug (W6) · Yuki, Priya · S-33 spaces, OKRs list and diagram · METHOD §2.9, §2.10**
The Growth team forms, with Yuki and four people from Product and Marketing. Priya creates the Growth space, and Yuki drafts G1 in the list.
- **The first key result.** Nobody knows the self-serve trial-to-paid rate, so G1's only key result is a baseline, G1.1. It passes every block-level check, with a due date and an owner, so G1 goes live at once.
- **The reason.** It is after the publication window, so it is marked added mid-cycle. Reasons are required (NW-Q2-13), so she writes "New Growth team formed 9 August".
- **The parent.** She aligns G1 to C1 with the keyboard: "Move under…" in the node's menu, then C1.

*Test:* Given a space created on 9 August, when its first objective is created with a baseline key result, then it is live and marked added mid-cycle with its reason. Given the keyboard "Move under…", then it aligns to C1 without a pointer.

**NW-Q3-10 · 16 Aug (W7) · Daniel · S-15 check-in, S-21 · METHOD §3.6**

**NW-Q3-11 · 23 Aug (W8) · Amara, Yuki · S-14 drawer · METHOD §2.10, §2.9**

|---|---|---|---|
| C2.1 Support cost per account | $101 | 0.89 | The questionnaire surge in Q2 delayed the tooling by six weeks |
| C6.1 Operating margin | August's 10.1% | 0.42 | The new price book reached renewals only on 1 September; most renewals fall in Q4 |

*Test:* Given the split format, when the review session runs, then it holds stages 1 to 4 only.

- **Process health.** The lowest statement is 1, "Our OKRs stayed visible and were genuinely used to make decisions", at 3.2. The summer left goals unread for weeks. The improvement action, owned by Elena: open the first ten minutes of every all-hands with the OKR diagram.
- **Close decisions:**

|---|---|
