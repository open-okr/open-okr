Ask in Slack, Microsoft Teams, WhatsApp and Telegram now answers. Every Ask replied "I have no language model configured, so I cannot answer that yet", whatever the workspace had set up, because the command recorded the question and never asked the copilot. It now goes through the copilot as the browser panel does: the answer, then the passages it stood on, numbered as the answer cites them, and the question and answer are kept in the member's copilot conversations. With AI off, the reply says why and lists the passages that match, as the panel does.
