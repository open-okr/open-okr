The Champion now offers to open a weekly session when its time comes, and to mark it skipped the day after if nobody opened it. Each offer rides on the session message the facilitator already gets, and waits in their review inbox: applying it opens or skips the session in their name, and dismissing it means it is not asked again. The Champion never changes a session itself, and in sandbox mode it records nothing. Opening or skipping the session any other way settles the offer. Monthly and quarterly reviews are unchanged.
