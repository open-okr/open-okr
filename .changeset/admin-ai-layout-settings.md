On the AI page, the assists (Features) share one Save changes button that lists which ones are not saved yet and saves only the ones that changed. Each tier under Tier routing is its own card with its own Save changes, and Back to the default sits in the card header. The Models and Budgets forms open from an Add a model or Set a budget button in the card header. The cards are grouped under Models, Tier routing, Spend and limits and Behaviour headings. Nothing about how routes, models, budgets or assists are stored has changed.
