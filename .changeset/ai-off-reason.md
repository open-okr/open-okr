When AI is off, the copilot now says why. A cloud workspace whose plan includes no AI credits is told so, with where to choose a plan or add its own key, rather than "No AI provider is configured". The same goes for egress controls that let nothing reach the provider, and for a model with no price. The AI admin page says when the plan carries no credits, and links to the Plan page.
