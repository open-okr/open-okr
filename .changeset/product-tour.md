A first sign-in now offers a product tour on whatever screen it lands, rather than a card only on the Work Map that most members never opened. Start tour shows one step at a time with the step's area lit and the rest dimmed: Home, Updates, Goals, the cycle strip while planning, Search, and Ask AI when the copilot can answer. It ends with Start drafting OKRs, which opens the cycle page. Skip tour, or Escape, ends it for good; Take the tour again in the avatar menu starts it once more.
