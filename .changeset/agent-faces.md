The agents have faces. The OKR Coach is blue, the OKR Champion is green, and the copilot and any custom agent wear a face in the text colour that follows light and dark. A face shows a mood that fits what the agent is saying: a sad Champion on an overdue check-in, a thinking Coach on a quality question, a happy one when every check passes. Faces appear on Agents and runs, the copilot, the inbox, the goal page, the Draft Coach, the alignment panel and the method pages. An idle face blinks and glances on a slow loop; the other moods play on hover and when the mood changes, and loop only while an agent is working. Every face holds still for anyone who asks for reduced motion.
