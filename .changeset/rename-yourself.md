You can change your own name on Your account. It changes the name this workspace shows and the name your account carries into the next workspace you join; other workspaces keep the name they have, because their administrators may have set it. An administrator can still rename another member, which changes that workspace only. Members a directory manages can rename themselves too, because directory sync never writes a name. Demonstration accounts keep their name. `people.updateOwnProfile` takes `name`, and the audit row says the name changed without saying what to.
