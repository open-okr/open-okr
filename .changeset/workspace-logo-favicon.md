Admin, Branding takes a logo and a favicon. A chosen image is previewed in a light and a dark sidebar, or a browser tab, before it is saved. The logo replaces the initials in the sidebar for every member and the favicon is the tab icon on signed-in screens. New action `settings.setWorkspaceBrandingImage`; the colour save now keeps the images.
