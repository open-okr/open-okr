Goals: every icon-only control says what it does in a tooltip on hover and on keyboard focus: check in, open, more actions, show or hide key results, the drag handle, add cycle, and the List, Diagram and Tree displays. An objective's drag handle lines up with its key results' handles, and its collapse arrow moves to the right of its actions. `Tooltip` is new in the kit.
