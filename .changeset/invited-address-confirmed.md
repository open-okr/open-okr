A person who signs up through a personal invitation, with the address it was sent to, no longer has to confirm that address: opening the invitation proved the inbox. They land in the workspace at once, where before they were asked to confirm by mail first. An account that is already signed in and accepts a personal invitation to its own address is confirmed too. A shared workspace link names no address and confirms nothing. Recorded on the instance chain as a confirmed address.
