Activity counts unread on the server, so its number no longer stops at the 50 rows a page holds, and the Updates badge says what it adds up ("3 to do, 19 unread"). A check-in group in Activity is named after its objective. A Board card can be deleted, after a confirmation. The first-visit tour outlines its stop with a dashed line that does not read as keyboard focus, scrolls the stop into view, and shows no Back on the first stop.
