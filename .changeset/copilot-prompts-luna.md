The copilot reads its answers more carefully on GPT-6 Luna, the default model on OpenRouter. It no longer reads somebody else's check-in or objective back to you as yours: when the passages do not show which is yours, it says so. When it has prepared a change card, its answer says so first instead of saying the information is missing. It no longer proposes work behind a different key result from the one you named. Answers may use bold, lists and inline code, and never headings, tables or links.
