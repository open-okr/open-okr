The Practice page follows the settings layout. The profile card applies a profile at once with its own button, and every setting below is saved together through one sticky bar with Discard. A value saved back to its profile's value clears the override instead of storing a copy. Applying a profile waits until unsaved changes are saved or discarded. The profile comparison marks each value that differs from Recommended with a highlight instead of tinting whole cells, on columns of equal width.
