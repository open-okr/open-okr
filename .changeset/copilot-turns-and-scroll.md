The copilot's conversation reads like a current chat. Your question is a bubble on the right, and the copilot's answer is prose across the panel under its face, the same while it arrives and once it is saved, so the answer no longer jumps or blinks when it finishes. A change the copilot proposes sits in the turn that asked for it, not in a block after the whole conversation. The panel follows the answer as it is written unless you scroll up to read something, and Jump to latest takes you back. An opened conversation starts at its newest turn. Coming back to an answer still being written shows it once, with any card it made, rather than as two answers.
