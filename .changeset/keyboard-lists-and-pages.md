The list keys reach more screens: `j` and `k` walk the OKRs list, the board's cards, the sessions list and the people directory, and `e` puts the caret in an objective's or key result's title. `[` and `]` move to the previous or next objective in its cycle, and to the previous or next task on its space's board, with links that say so for anybody not using the keyboard.
