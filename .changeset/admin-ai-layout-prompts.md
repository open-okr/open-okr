On the AI page, each prompt is its own card with its own footer: Discard, Save a new version, and Restore the built-in on the left. A new version that fails its evaluation set is still refused, and the footer names the checks it failed. Privacy and egress saves through its own footer with Discard. The external agent calls and ratings lists scroll inside their cards, and they sit under a new Usage by agents and members heading. Nothing about how prompts, privacy settings or usage are stored has changed.
