An instance can now name its own source code link, terms, privacy policy and support address, with `OPENOKR_SOURCE_URL`, `OPENOKR_TERMS_URL`, `OPENOKR_PRIVACY_URL` and `OPENOKR_SUPPORT_EMAIL`. The sign-up page shows the terms and privacy links once they are set. Support access now uses the instance's own name rather than "OpenOKR", and the account menu's link reads "Source code".
