One instance can now run a workspace per company. With `OPENOKR_CLOUD=true` and no plans there is no plan screen and no seat limit, a workspace from before the flag appears in the console within the hour, and `OPENOKR_AI_ALLOW_PRIVATE_ENDPOINTS` lets a group use its own AI server. The console's usage figures now refresh on their own. See `docs/install/group.md`.
