Goals: each objective is a card with its progress across it and its key results under a band naming the columns. Values read as text with a dotted underline until they are used, the due date reads "31 Dec 2026 in 59 days", a failing rule shows its words and a link to the rule, and stop and delete wait in each row's menu. The toolbar is scope tabs with counts, one Filter button whose panel has a row per question and leaves the filters in force as chips, the display as icons, and Export as a menu. On a phone titles wrap in full and every row shows its progress. `TextArea` passes a `ref` to its textarea.
