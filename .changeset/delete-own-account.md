A person can delete their own account from the foot of Your account. Confirmed with their password, or a fresh sign-in for an account with none, it signs them out everywhere and runs 14 days later; signing in again before then keeps the account. When it runs, every workspace they belong to erases them as an administrator's erasure does, what they wrote stays under a placeholder name, each workspace's administrators and their manager are told what now needs an owner, and the account is anonymised. Refused for demonstration accounts, members a directory manages, and the last owner of any workspace. Migration 0167 adds `account_deletions`; the scheduler runs due deletions every hour, and `pnpm account:delete-due` runs them now.
