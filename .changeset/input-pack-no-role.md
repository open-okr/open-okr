A member who has no role can no longer change a cycle's input pack. Before, such a member (as the demo personas and some older or imported members are) was treated as holding edit on the workspace and could mark items and confirm distribution. Only the Owner, an Admin, a role that grants edit on the workspace, or the cycle's facilitator with a Member role or above may change it, as the design says. The cycle page shows the pack read only to a member with no role.
