The copilot now proposes an initiative or a task behind a key result in any space you may edit, also when somebody else owns the objective. It used to offer only the key results of objectives you could edit, so a member who works in a space without owning its objective never got one. Its choices now come from every cycle being planned or running, the running quarter first, which is the rule the Board, the initiative screens and the objective page already use. Those pickers also put this quarter ahead of next quarter when neither has been started yet; before, next quarter came first.
