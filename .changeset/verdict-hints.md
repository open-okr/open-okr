A rule named by its key explains itself where it is named. Resting on a verdict such as "KR-3" on a goal's coach strip, on "Read the rule" in the goals list, or on a nudge's rule on the review inbox and the nudge volume page shows the rule's name, what kind it is, what a check looks for or when a nudge fires and who hears it. A click still opens the rule's page, and a quality check's page now links to its exact section of the method.
