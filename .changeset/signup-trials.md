A cloud can offer trials at sign-up: with `OPENOKR_CLOUD_TRIAL_DAYS` and `OPENOKR_CLOUD_TRIAL_PLAN` both set, a workspace somebody signs up for starts on that catalogue plan for that many days, and the hourly trial sweep ends it. Either unset, or a plan the catalogue does not hold, starts no trial. A workspace on a trial counts as one of its owner's free workspaces.
