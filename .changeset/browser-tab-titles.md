Each browser tab now names its screen before the instance's name, such as "KPIs · OKRGoal" and "Branding · OKRGoal". The page of one objective, KPI, initiative, task, person, space, session or document shows that item's own name. A reader who may not open the item sees only its kind. Before this, every tab read the instance's name alone.
