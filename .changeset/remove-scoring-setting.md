Admin, Practice no longer offers a "Scoring" on or off switch. It never did anything: turning it off changed nothing, and nothing in the method said what off should mean. A workspace that had turned it off loses nothing, and the switch is dropped from its stored settings the next time any practice setting is saved. Key results are scored at the close as before.
