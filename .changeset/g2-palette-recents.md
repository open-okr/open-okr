The command palette lists what you opened lately first, and a tab row narrows it to objectives, key results, KPIs, initiatives, tasks or people. Recents are kept in your browser, per device. A thing you can no longer open, or that was deleted, is left out. On a new device the list starts from what you changed last. A recent match is lifted in the results, never above an exact title match.
