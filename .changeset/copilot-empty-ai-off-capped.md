The copilot panel is more useful when it is empty, when AI is off and when it is capped. An empty panel offers four questions to ask with one press. With AI off, the passages that match a question and the reason no answer was written stay on screen after the turn ends, rather than vanishing, because with AI off they are the answer. Whoever can turn AI on is linked to the page that does it: the AI settings, the plan, or their own AI keys where the workspace takes them. A capped copilot says when it can answer again. And a failure to check whether the copilot can answer now makes the copilot say it cannot answer right now, instead of taking every page of the workspace down with it.
