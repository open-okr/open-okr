Mention the OKR Coach in a comment on a goal and it now reviews the goal and replies in the same thread. The review is the method's quality checks on the objective and its key results, so it works with no AI provider: each point names the check, what it saw and what to do, and cites its rule. Whoever asked is told the reply is there, under the usual reminder rules, so a second ping about the same goal on the same day waits. The comment box's `@` now offers the Coach. The Coach reads only goals it is bound to, says nothing about one it cannot see, and posts nothing while it is turned off or in sandbox mode.
