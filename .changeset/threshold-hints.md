Every threshold on Admin, Rhythm and thresholds has a small "i". Where its reason cites a principle by number, such as "(principle 5)", the hint says what that principle is. It also names the part of the method the threshold comes from and links to that exact section. Check-in frequency and the anchor day, which show no reason on the card, carry theirs in the hint.
