With billing on, an administrator can now buy and change a plan on Admin, Plan and seats, monthly or yearly, at the prices and in the currency the provider set in Stripe. More seats starts now and Stripe charges the difference. Fewer seats waits for the end of the period, and is refused below the headcount. Cards, invoices and cancelling are under Manage billing. See `docs/install/cloud.md`.
