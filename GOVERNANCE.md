# Governance

How decisions are made in the OpenOKR project.

## The short version

While the project is young, the project owner has the final say on everything: scope, design, the OKR method canon, releases, and what merges. This is deliberate. A coherent product needs one accountable decision-maker until there is a community large enough to share that load.

## Roles

| Role | Who | What they decide |
|---|---|---|
| Project owner | The founding maintainer | Final say on all decisions while the project is young |
| Maintainers | Appointed by the owner | Review and merge changes, triage issues |
| Contributors | Anyone | Propose changes through issues and pull requests |

## How decisions happen

1. Anyone can open an issue to propose or question anything.
2. Discussion happens in the open, on the issue.
3. A maintainer or the owner decides. Contested decisions escalate to the owner, whose decision is final.

Two areas are held more tightly than the rest:

- **The method canon.** `docs/development-plan/METHOD.md` defines the OKR practice the product enforces. Changes to its rules, thresholds and coaching messages are owner decisions, always.
- **Licensing.** The licence (AGPL-3.0) and the contributor licence agreement can only change by owner decision.

## The promise

As the community grows, governance widens: more maintainers, documented decision areas they own outright, and a written process for disputes. What will not change silently is the licence, the contributor agreement, or this document. Changes to any of them are announced, not slipped in.
